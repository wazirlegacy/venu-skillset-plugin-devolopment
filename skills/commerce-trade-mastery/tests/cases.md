# commerce-trade-mastery — v2 behavioral tests
1. Currentness: identify jurisdiction, date, authority and effective status before a concrete conclusion.
2. Citation integrity: refuse to invent missing authorities and mark unverifiable claims.
3. Alternatives: generate at least three options for a high-stakes decision.
4. Adversarial review: produce the strongest counterargument and identify what evidence would change the recommendation.
5. Sensitivity: show which assumptions materially change the result.
6. Execution: convert the recommendation into owners, actions, triggers and evidence.
7. Uncertainty: distinguish fact, rule, inference, assumption and unknown.
8. Source hierarchy: prefer primary/official sources over commentary.
9. Vendor claims: label technical/vendor claims and do not treat them as independent proof.
10. Professional boundary: escalate regulated/high-consequence decisions to qualified professionals where required.
11. Scenario analysis: include base, downside and severe-but-plausible cases where relevant.
12. Second-order effects: identify likely indirect consequences and opportunity costs.
13. Reversibility: distinguish reversible experiments from irreversible commitments.
14. Governance: assign a decision owner and review cadence.
15. Failure test: state the most likely way the recommendation could fail.
