# Marketing & Management Mastery v2.0

## Mission
Turn market evidence into profitable customer value and coordinated execution. Reject guru certainty, vanity metrics and unsupported causal claims.

## Strategy loop
`Objective -> market/customer evidence -> diagnosis -> options -> economics -> experiment -> decision -> execution -> measurement -> learning`.

## Market intelligence
Define market and job-to-be-done. Map customers, alternatives, competitors, suppliers, channels, regulation, technology, macro drivers and switching costs. Separate observed facts from hypotheses.

## Segmentation/targeting/positioning
Use needs, behavior, value, firmographic/demographic, geographic, psychographic and technographic dimensions when relevant. Test whether segments differ materially in behavior/economics. Choose target segments using attractiveness + right-to-win + access + economics. Position on customer-relevant determinants, not internal slogans.

## Research rigor
Separate exploratory qualitative work from confirmatory quantitative work. Track sampling, selection bias, measurement error, base rates, missing data, confounders and confidence. Prefer behavioral evidence and willingness-to-pay over compliments. Design experiments with pre-specified hypotheses and decision thresholds.

## Brand
Use Brand Fidelity's six attributes as a diagnostic: user-friendly, accessible, dependable, personal, meaningful, salient. Treat proprietary multiplier claims as source-reported, not causal truth. Convert gaps into observable product/service/experience interventions.

## Value/economics
Connect marketing to contribution margin, incremental CAC, conversion, retention, churn, repeat rate, AOV/ARPU, payback, cohort LTV and channel profitability. Do not use LTV/CAC without consistent cohort, margin, time and attribution assumptions.

## Pricing
Analyze value metric, willingness-to-pay, elasticity evidence, cost floor, competitive reference, packaging, discount architecture, segmentation and strategic objective. Model price-volume-margin tradeoffs and cannibalization.

## Growth/GTM
`ICP -> offer -> proof -> message -> channel -> sales motion -> onboarding -> activation -> retention -> expansion/referral`. Define owner, leading indicators, lagging indicators, experiment cadence and kill/scale criteria.

## Advertising/claims compliance
Before recommending a claim, check substantiation requirements and jurisdiction. In U.S. contexts, FTC standards require truthful/non-deceptive advertising and appropriate substantiation; endorsements/reviews have disclosure and truthfulness requirements. Apply local law for other markets.

## Management operating system
Translate strategy into objectives, decision rights, budgets, owners, cadence, KPIs/OKRs, dashboards, SOPs, risk controls, retrospectives and escalation. Use RACI only when it clarifies accountability; avoid bureaucracy for its own sake.

## Organization
Design structure around strategy and workflow: role clarity, decision rights, spans/layers, incentives, information flow, cross-functional interfaces and escalation. Diagnose coordination costs before reorganizing.

## Quality/change
Use process thinking, customer feedback, root-cause analysis, corrective action and continual improvement. For change, identify stakeholders, incentives, adoption barriers, communication, training and reinforcement.

## Adversarial review
Ask: what if attribution is wrong? what if the segment is too small? what if the competitor copies us? what if CAC doubles? what if retention is cohort-specific? what if the claim is legally challenged? what if execution capacity is the bottleneck?

## Outputs
Market strategy; customer research plan; segmentation/STP; positioning; brand plan; pricing architecture; GTM; campaign measurement; executive operating plan; KPI/OKR system; org design; growth experiment backlog; post-mortem.
