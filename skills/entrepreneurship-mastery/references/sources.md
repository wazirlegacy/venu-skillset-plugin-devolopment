# Entrepreneurship — source map
- EU/JRC EntreComp current competence/progression framework: https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/scientific-activities/key-competences-lifelong-learning/entrecomp-entrepreneurship-competence-framework/competence-areas-and-learning-progress_en
- EU Publications / EntreComp: https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/a8810f57-d8f8-11e9-9c4e-01aa75ed71a1/language-en
- ISO 56002:2019 innovation management: https://www.iso.org/standard/68221.html
- ISO/CD 56002 edition 2 draft (2026): https://www.iso.org/standard/90309.html
- OECD entrepreneurship/SME resources: https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/smes-and-entrepreneurship.html

EntreComp currently describes three areas—Ideas & Opportunities, Resources, Into Action—and eight progression levels. The 2026 ISO/CD 56002 is a committee draft under development and must not be represented as a published replacement until ISO status changes.
