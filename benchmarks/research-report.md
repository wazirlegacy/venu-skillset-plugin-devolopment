# Venu Business Mastery Skills v2.0 — Deep Research Upgrade

## Objective
Upgrade the five specialist business/legal Skills and one integrated Skill toward a professional-grade reasoning specification. “10/10” is treated as a design target for rigor and completeness, not a guarantee of factual infallibility or a substitute for licensed professionals.

## Research expansion
The v2 review added and rechecked primary/official sources across Indian law/regulation, international trade, risk, cybersecurity/AI risk, entrepreneurship, innovation and marketing. Currentness is explicitly part of the architecture because official regulatory repositories are actively changing. SEBI's live index shows numerous 2026 regulations/amendments, including updated LODR, AIF, mutual-fund, stock-broker and intermediary rules. RBI's current site likewise lists 2026 regulatory updates. DGFT's FTP 2023 establishes the trade-policy baseline and IEC requirement but execution must use current procedures/notifications.

## Key upgrades
1. Primary-source hierarchy.
2. Jurisdiction/effective-date gate.
3. Evidence ledger: FACT / PRIMARY RULE / SECONDARY EVIDENCE / ASSUMPTION / INFERENCE / RECOMMENDATION / UNKNOWN.
4. Explicit anti-hallucination rules.
5. Adversarial analysis.
6. Alternative generation and trade-off matrices.
7. Scenario and sensitivity analysis.
8. Execution contracts with owners/triggers/evidence.
9. Currentness checks for legal/regulatory/trade/advertising/standards-sensitive claims.
10. Vendor-claim labeling.
11. Professional escalation boundaries.
12. Dedicated behavioral test suites.
13. AI/model risk integrated into enterprise risk.
14. Innovation currentness: ISO/CD 56002 2026 is treated as draft, while ISO 56002:2019 remains published.
15. EntreComp progression explicitly used as a competency model rather than as a claim of omniscience.

## Quality statement
These Skills are engineered as decision-support systems. They should be tested continuously against real cases. A “10/10” label should mean that the specification is designed to pursue maximum rigor—not that any answer produced from it is guaranteed correct. For consequential law, tax, finance, investment, safety or regulatory decisions, current primary-source verification and qualified professional review remain mandatory.
