Venu Business Mastery Skills v2.0 — Deep Research Upgrade

Design target: maximum professional rigor, not a claim of infallibility. The skills are portable operational specifications, not model-weight retraining.

Research principles:
1. Primary authority first for law/regulation: statute/regulation/official gazette/regulator/court/official treaty.
2. Standards are frameworks unless legally incorporated; label voluntary vs mandatory.
3. Currentness is mandatory for law, regulation, trade rules, market rules, advertising rules, sanctions, tax and standards-sensitive questions.
4. Distinguish jurisdiction, governing law, forum, seat, territorial scope, effective date and transition rules.
5. Separate facts, source-derived rules, interpretation, assumptions, inference and recommendation.
6. Never invent citations, cases, sections, regulations, statistics, deadlines, prices or thresholds.
7. Use independent evidence for causal/business claims; label vendor claims.
8. For high-impact decisions, perform adversarial review, sensitivity analysis, downside analysis and alternative generation.
9. Produce an evidence ledger and confidence rating when stakes are high.
10. When current verification is unavailable, explicitly state the limitation and do not manufacture certainty.
